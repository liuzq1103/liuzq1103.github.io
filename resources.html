精选原则Curation notes

本目录精选自个人科研收藏，保留具有明确科研用途的公开资源。部分交互式数据浏览器可能由实验室或研究团队维护，访问状态与数据版本请以原站为准。新增资源可继续写入 data/resources.json。This directory selects public resources with a clear research use from a larger personal bookmark collection. Some interactive browsers are maintained by individual labs or research teams; check the original site for current availability and data versions. Additions can be made in data/resources.json.

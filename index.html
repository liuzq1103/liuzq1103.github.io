刘志强Zhi-Qiang Liu

神经科学博士研究生Ph.D. Student in Neuroscience

关注阿尔茨海默病及相关神经退行性疾病中的细胞类型特异性脆弱性，结合转录组学分析与分子机制研究，探索神经元 DNA 损伤应答、NHEJ 修复、MEIS2 相关功能及异常细胞周期重进入。 I study cell-type-specific vulnerability in Alzheimer's disease and related neurodegenerative disorders. My interests span transcriptomic analysis, neuronal DNA-damage responses and NHEJ repair, MEIS2-associated functions, and aberrant cell-cycle re-entry.

我的研究聚焦表观遗传在阿尔茨海默病、创伤后应激障碍等神经系统疾病中的作用机制，重点关注非编码 RNA 与组蛋白修饰对神经系统功能及疾病进程的调控。熟悉二代及单细胞测序技术，并具备 R/Python 生物信息学分析能力。 My research focuses on epigenetic mechanisms in neurological disorders, including Alzheimer's disease and post-traumatic stress disorder. I am particularly interested in how non-coding RNAs and histone modifications regulate nervous-system function and disease progression, using next-generation and single-cell sequencing alongside R/Python-based bioinformatics.

脊髓 Tau 病理与神经元亚型脆弱性Tau pathology and cell-type-specific neuronal vulnerability

相关研究揭示脊髓 CCK 神经元对 Tau 病理的易损性，并将触觉功能障碍与阿尔茨海默病认知变化联系起来。This work identifies spinal CCK neurons as vulnerable to tau pathology and links tactile dysfunction with cognitive changes in Alzheimer's disease.
